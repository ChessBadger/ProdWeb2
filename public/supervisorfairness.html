Crew quality baseline is recency-weighted (90-day half-life), and job crew quality excludes the supervisor where possible.

Adjusted effects use fixed-effects models with store + month demeaning, controls for store size and crew size, and clustered standard errors (by store).

Significance includes clustered p-values plus bootstrap/permutation checks for the selected supervisor.
