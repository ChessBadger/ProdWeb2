SCENARIO AND LABOR SEMANTICS
- The integrated full-onsite model remains the default and its weights are unchanged.
- Uncheck its toggle for the original attendance-proxy reference. Stores without mapped
  full history retain that explicitly labelled reference fallback instead of disappearing.
- Modas/latest-account, shrink, supervisor/RX contribution, training and early/late
  controls reuse the original rate resolver and minimum-role-factor/shrink rules.
- Manual integrated scenarios are: unchanged integrated estimate + (original reference
  under assumptions minus its default reference). These are transparent assumption-based
  scenarios, NOT a retrained/independently validated predictor or causal staffing effect.
- The original reference retains its validated code's bounded crew adjustments and may
  show little/no duration change where missing crew evidence prevents a reliable comparison.
- Labor Hours is duration x full headcount, the original duration-model definition.
  Effective Counting Hours applies counting-contribution factors; it is not actual payroll
  or attendance labor. Early/late percentages are contribution assumptions, not clock entries.
- 100% shrink retains the original 1% numerical floor. All-zero contribution crews are rejected.
- Production rows show rank, rate, latest-account arrows, adjusted rate, projected pieces
  and evidence/fallback labels. Projections are not promised actual counts.
- Goal deltas/recommendations and hypothetical one-person staffing comparisons are labelled.
